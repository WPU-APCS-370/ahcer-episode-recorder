Privacy Policy

AHC Episode App (AHCER-App)

Effective Date: 1.1.2026

1. Who We Are

The AHC Episode App (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is operated by E-AHC / AHCER to support individuals and families affected by Alternating Hemiplegia of Childhood (AHC).

The app is designed to help patients, parents, guardians, and caregivers track health episodes, symptoms, and medications for personal care management and, where applicable, to support aggregated research, platform improvement, and rare disease insight efforts.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:
ahcappE@gmail.com

2. Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Patients whose health information is recorded in the app
  • Parents or legal guardians who manage accounts on behalf of minors
  • Caregivers authorized by a family
  • Registered users of the AHC Episode App

This policy applies only to the app and its related services, not to third-party websites or services linked from the app.

3. Information We Collect

  • Health Information (sensitive data)

  • We may collect:

  • Episode start and end times
  • Episode duration
  • Neurological and physical symptoms
  • Medication names, dosages, schedules, and rescue medications
  • Caregiver notes and observations
  • Dates and frequency of episodes

  • Account & User Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Login credentials
  • User role and relationship to the patient

  • Technical & Usage Information

  • IP address
  • Device and browser type
  • Login timestamps
  • Error logs and performance data

We do not use advertising trackers and do not serve advertisements.

  • How We Use Information

  • Provide episode tracking and medication management tools
  • Allow families and caregivers to review patterns and history
  • Maintain, secure, and support user accounts
  • Improve app functionality and reliability
  • Respond to support requests

4. Research and Sustainability Use

The app may use aggregated and de-identified data to support rare disease research, platform improvement, and long-term sustainability. This may include providing paid access to analytical insights, reports, or platform functionality for universities, researchers, nonprofit organizations, or industry partners. No identifiable or individual-level health data is shared for these purposes without explicit consent.

We do not sell personal or identifiable health data.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the EU or UK, processing is based on:

  • Explicit consent
  • Health management purposes
  • Legitimate interests in maintaining a secure service

Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

6. Children’s Privacy & Parental Rights

  • Accounts for minors must be managed by a parent or legal guardian
  • Parents act as data controllers for their child’s data
  • We do not knowingly allow minors to independently create accounts

7. HIPAA Considerations (U.S.)

The app is a patient- and caregiver-managed health tracking tool. We apply HIPAA-aligned safeguards but are not a covered entity unless operating under specific agreements.

8. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share information only:

  • With authorized caregivers
  • With service providers under confidentiality agreements
  • When required by law

Research use is limited to aggregated or de-identified data unless explicit consent is obtained.

9. Data Ownership & Stewardship

  • Users retain ownership and control of their personal and health information.
  • The app operator acts as a data steward responsible for safeguarding data.
  • Stewardship does not confer ownership rights.
  • Stewardship responsibilities may be transitioned by agreement without affecting user rights.

10. Data Retention

We retain data while accounts remain active or as necessary to provide services. Users may request deletion at any time.

11. Your Rights

Depending on jurisdiction, users may:

  • Access their data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Request deletion
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Export data
  • Withdraw consent

12. Security

We use reasonable safeguards including:

  • Secure authentication
  • Encrypted connections
  • Role-based access controls

13. International Data Transfers

Where applicable, international transfers follow appropriate safeguards.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes in stewardship or organizational structure will not reduce user privacy protections.
